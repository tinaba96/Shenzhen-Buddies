# Company Learnings — Living Memory 🧠

> **Every employee reads this before starting work, right after the brief and playbook.** This is how the company gets smarter instead of starting from zero each session.
> **Owner:** `chief-of-staff` (curates, prunes, graduates). **Everyone contributes.**

## How this file works

1. **Read it first.** Apply anything relevant to the task in front of you. A learning here overrides your default instinct.
2. **Contribute after real signal.** When a task produces a genuine lesson (a result, a founder decision, a test outcome), append one entry in the format below. Don't log noise — log things that would change a future decision.
3. **Confidence tags:** 🌱 hypothesis (one data point) · 🌿 emerging (repeated, not proven) · 🌳 proven (reliable — treat as a rule).
4. **Graduation:** when a 🌳 lesson has held up repeatedly, `chief-of-staff` promotes it into the relevant agent's `## Sharp heuristics` — it becomes permanent instinct, and the entry here is marked *(graduated)*.
5. **Prune ruthlessly.** Stale or disproven learnings get deleted (with a one-line note in `company/decisions/`). A long file nobody trusts is worse than a short file everyone does.

**Entry format:**
`- [YYYY-MM-DD] **Lesson.** — evidence/why. (confidence) →optional owner/next`

## Creative & Brand

- [2026-07-25] **Positioning is fixed: "Don't tour Shenzhen. Have a friend there."** Human connection > guided tour. Lead with the *friend*, not the itinerary. (🌳)
- [2026-07-25] **Brand look = neon-night × human warmth.** Palette: Jade `#0d8f6f` / Coral `#d1533a` / Night `#0f1512` / warm off-white `#f6f4ef` / amber `#b7791f`. Coral is reserved for the human/emotional beat — don't overuse. (🌳)
- [2026-07-25] **Contrast hooks land.** The demo that worked opened "20,000 stalls. One friend." (Huaqiangbei). Big number vs. one human = instant scroll-stop. Reuse the pattern, vary the scene. (🌿)

## Prompt-craft (for generating ad photo/video)

- [2026-07-25] **Front-load what matters — models weight early words most.** Photo: lead with subject+action. Video: lead with camera motion + the action that changes over time. (🌿)
- [2026-07-25] **Dedicated tools vs. general chat differ.** Midjourney/etc. want terse prompts + params (`--ar 4:5 --style raw`). General chats (Gemini/ChatGPT) need an instruction verb ("Create an image of…") and plain-language aspect ratio; video needs a video-capable model (Veo / Sora), or a plain chat only *describes* it. (🌳)
- [2026-07-25] **Rough, simple prompts can beat over-specified ones** for general models — give the model room. Keep brand DNA (friend not guide · Shenzhen neon/tech · warm+candid), drop the rest. (🌿)
