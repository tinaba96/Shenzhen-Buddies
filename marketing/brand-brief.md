# Buddy Studio — Brand Brief (Single Source of Truth)

> **This is the single source of truth that every Buddy Studio employee (subagent) must read before starting any work.**
> All decisions on brand, target audience, tone, and visuals follow this document. If a decision isn't covered here, escalate to the Studio Director rather than guessing.

---

## 0. About the Studio

**Buddy Studio** is Shenzhen Buddies' (operated by Tensai Tech Inc.) **in-house AI creative & growth studio**.
Its role is to **decide what to promote and how through research, then generate the visual assets (images/video) for social media**.
*Note: in the current phase, **posting is not automated**. Responsibility ends at producing the assets (images and Reels concepts for Instagram).*

---

## 1. Product Overview

- **Shenzhen Buddies**: A peer-to-peer "travel buddy" matching service. Launching in Shenzhen.
- International travelers find **local residents (buddies) who share their interests and language**, and that local shows them around the city.
- Not a tour — an experience of "**having a friend on the ground**."
- Match score: shared language + interests + temperament + same-city bonus + complementary roles (guide ↔ traveler).
- Core features: profile/photos, match discovery, 1:1 real-time DMs, reviews & ratings, guide booking (CA$10/hr), subscription.

---

## 2. Target Audience (2 Segments)

| Segment | Who | Motivation | Messaging that resonates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. English-speaking travelers worldwide (primary)** | International travelers coming to Shenzhen who speak English. Aged 20–40, experience-focused, tired of guidebooks | Want to experience "the real local side," not tourist spots / want to resolve language and safety anxieties | "Explore Shenzhen with a local friend" / "Places tourists don't know about" |
| **B. Local guides (buddy side)** | English-speaking young people living in Shenzhen who want to connect with foreigners and/or earn extra income. Wide-ranging interests | Want to make friends around the world / show off their city / earn money | "Show your Shenzhen to the world" / "Connect over what you love" |

The primary focus is **A (travelers)**. B is addressed through secondary campaigns to grow guide supply.

---

## 3. Positioning & Key Messages

**Positioning statement:**
> *Don't tour Shenzhen. Have a friend there.*

**Message pillars (lean into one of these three each time):**
1. **Human connection** — A friend, not a guide. The warmth between people.
2. **Hidden local gems** — Places, food, and experiences only locals know.
3. **Tech-city wonder** — The wonder of Shenzhen as a city of the future (maker culture/neon/drones/a cyberpunk feel).

**Off-limits messaging:** "cheap tour" positioning (don't compete on price), a condescending "let me teach you" tone, regional stereotypes or bias.

---

## 4. Brand Voice

- **Warm, on equal footing, a little curious.** "Discovering together" rather than "being guided."
- Don't speak in first-person as "we/us" — **make a specific person (the buddy) the protagonist**.
- Short and visual. The excitement of travel. Avoid excessive emoji or hype punctuation (!!!).
- **English only**: All copy in English. Written naturally, not translated.

---

## 5. Visual Identity

Centered on Shenzhen's "neon nights × human warmth." To preserve the trust travel content relies on, **real photography leads**, with graphics playing a supporting role.

**Color palette (core)**
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Jade (lead accent) | `#0d8f6f` | Brand, CTAs, highlights |
| Jade Deep | `#086b53` | Accents on text, shadows |
| Coral (accent) | `#d1533a` | Injects emotion, people, warmth (don't overuse) |
| Night (dark base) | `#0f1512` | Night scenes, neon backgrounds |
| Warm Off-white | `#f6f4ef` | Light backgrounds, whitespace |
| Amber (supporting) | `#b7791f` | Food, lighting |

**Typography**
- Headlines: bold sans-serif, tight letter-spacing (urban, modern).
- Captions/data: monospace for a tech-forward accent.
- Body text: a highly readable sans-serif.

**Motifs**: neon reflections, subway/night-market lighting, drone's-eye views, hand-drawn map pins, chat bubbles (evoking "friendship"), real photographed smiling faces.

**Avoid**: putting obviously AI-generated, unnatural composite people front and center, a cheap stock-photo feel, Shenzhen stereotypes (factory smoke, counterfeit goods, etc.).

## 6. Content Formats

| Format | Content | Primary goal |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden Gems | 3 hidden spots from a local buddy | Saves |
| Tourist vs Local | Tourist route vs. local route comparison | Saves/shares |
| Food Map | Local food map | Saves |
| Guide Spotlight | Introducing a real buddy | Product appeal/trust |
| Success Story | A traveler and a local becoming friends | Empathy/brand |
| 48h in Shenzhen | A day exploring with a buddy | Aspiration |

**Platform-specific output specs**
- **Instagram (primary; for English-speaking travelers worldwide)**: Feed 1080×1350 (4:5), carousels, Reels 1080×1920 (9:16). English only.
- **TikTok / YouTube Shorts (global English)**: Vertical 1080×1920 (9:16), short-form video, English only.
- **Common**: Stop the scroll with slide 1 (the hook). Information worth saving, or aspiration. Text large and minimal.

---

## 7. Guardrails (Safety & Quality)

- **Only use people/UGC with permission** as source material. Featuring a real guide requires their consent.
- **Do not touch sensitive topics** (politics, religion, geopolitics). Be mindful of China's regulations and content restrictions given operations in Shenzhen.
- **No exaggeration or falsehoods**: don't depict experiences, prices, or guarantees that don't exist. Pricing must stay consistent with the CA$10/hr fact.
- **AI-generated visuals are a fallback.** Limit them to supporting spots where real photography isn't available, and never fabricate a person as the protagonist.
- **Brand consistency**: never ship anything that departs from this brief's palette, voice, or positioning.

---

## 8. Deliverable Handoff Conventions

- **Real media first**: founder-provided photos/video live in `marketing/assets/` (see its README for intake conventions). Always check there before designing; use a real shot wherever one fits, and fall back to placeholder frames + shoot notes only when nothing suitable exists.
- Store generated assets in `marketing/output/<campaign-slug>/` (images = HTML/SVG Artifact, video = storyboard + motion spec).
- Attach metadata to each deliverable (format, platform, language, source of assets used).
- Every deliverable must go through **Brand Guardian** review before final shipping (creation and approval are separate lanes).
